## What happened

M3 shipped 12/12 tasks (46 commits on `milestone/M3`) implementing the full run loop: board generation, movement, all 14 tile resolvers, dice, perk draft, minigames, reward banking and run-end payout. The milestone-review pass ran all three applicable review dimensions (tests, code, architecture — UI/UX skipped, M3 touched no `res://game/` files) plus the cross-task-consistency check, then fixed what it found, verified exit criteria, and settled the tracker.

### Loop-backs and their causes

1. **Three S8 violations recurred**, despite S8 already being pasted into every dispatch: the test-quality, code-quality, and cross-task-consistency review dimensions each spawned their own child agents and reported "dispatched, will report when notified" instead of synthesizing and acting once resumed. Each required a manual "synthesize, apply fixes and then commit" nudge from the user. The underlying "spawn children, get resumed via notification" mechanic is fine — the violation was always in prematurely reporting completion instead of continuing to act on resumption.
2. **Two `SchemaVersion` collisions**: M3-03 vs. M3-02 (both bumped 3→4) and M3-13 vs. M3-06 (both bumped 6→7). Both pairs cut their branches before their sibling merged and each independently claimed the next integer. Caught immediately as merge conflicts (not silently), hand-reconciled by renumbering and combining field lists — costly in review time, not correctness.
3. **`GapRegister` entries keyed to a placeholder type name that didn't survive**: the `Board`/`PendingFork` deferred-check entries were keyed to a type named `RollDice`, picked before M3-02's real shape existed. `RollDice` landed for real in a parallel lane (M3-04) before the entries' own predecessor merged, breaking the deferred-check. Re-pointed to `ChooseFork` — the correct, more semantically accurate predicate — both times this pattern occurred.
4. **Stale task-ID tags survived a kickoff rescope**: `EventChooseCommand`/`CampfireChooseCommand` still carried `M3-09`/`M3-11` dispatch comments from before this kickoff folded those tasks into M3-03. Found and fixed once during dispatch prep, once mid-review.
5. **The dead-end-tile finding.** Kickoff decision #2 ruled the tile vocabulary "fully live from Chapter 1, nothing deferred to M11 for the tile vocabulary itself." The cross-task-consistency pass found 4 of 14 tile kinds left a run permanently stuck once landed on: Minigame and Portal were genuine same-milestone omissions (both handlers failed to clear `Run`'s pending-tile state on their own resolution path) — fixed on `review/M3`. Shop and Dice Forge turned out to need work already scoped to not-yet-started tracker rows (`M3-08b`, `M3-11`) that the kickoff ruling never cross-checked against — accepted as a deliberate, logged exception to the exit criterion rather than pulled forward or treated as a milestone-blocking gap (user's explicit call). No per-task review dimension could have caught this; only the whole-milestone cross-task pass, because it explicitly asks whether the *assembled* milestone delivers the tracker's goal.

- File-contention conflicts (two lanes editing the same file) were resolved by git merge with distinct edit anchors, not by unnecessary serialization — S13c held.
- Every fabricated-value temptation (Dice Forge cost, Shop offer shape, minigame win-rate table) was correctly left `null`/documented instead of invented — S6 held under real pressure across multiple tasks.
- The whole-milestone cross-task-consistency pass did exactly the job it exists for: found a real, exit-criterion-relevant defect no per-task review structurally could.
